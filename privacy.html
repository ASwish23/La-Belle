Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how La Belle Nails & Relaxation ("La Belle", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website, book an appointment, or contact our salon at 440 Rayford Rd, Spring, TX 77386.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Online booking. When you book through our online scheduler, you provide details such as your name, phone number, email address, the service and time you choose, and any notes you add.
  • Phone and in-person contact. When you call or visit, we may note your name, phone number and appointment details.

Information collected automatically

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website. Like every website, the services that deliver parts of our pages (listed below) receive basic technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you are viewing, so they can send that content to you.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. When you first visit, we ask you to choose between Essential only and Accept all. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie Settings link at the bottom of every page.

Essential (always on)

  • Your cookie choice. We save your choice in your browser's local storage (under the name labelle-cookie-consent) so we don't ask again on every page. It never leaves your device.
  • Online booking (Setmore). Our appointment scheduler is provided by Setmore. When you open it, Setmore may set cookies needed to complete your booking.

Optional (only if you choose "Accept all" or click "Show map")

  • Google Maps. The map on our Appointments page is provided by Google, which may set cookies and collect usage information. Until you allow it, we show a placeholder instead and do not load the map.

Other third-party content

  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used on this site.
  • Unsplash hosts some of the photos on this site.

These services do not set cookies through our site, but they receive your IP address when your browser loads their content. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features, such as online booking, may not work without them.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, remind you about and manage your appointments.
  • To provide our services and answer your questions.
  • To keep our website working and secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Who we share information with

  • Service providers who help us run the salon and this website: Setmore (online booking), Google (maps and fonts) and Unsplash (images). They may use your information only to provide their services, under their own privacy policies, such as Google's Privacy Policy. Setmore's privacy policy is available on setmore.com.
  • Legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our clients, staff or business.
  • Business changes, if the salon is sold or transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

How long we keep information

We keep appointment and contact details for as long as you are a client and as needed for our records, legal and tax obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

How we protect information

We use reasonable measures to protect your information and rely on established providers for online booking. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live, including Texas and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have additional rights, such as the right to opt out of certain uses of your information or to appeal a decision we make about your request. To make a request, call us or visit the salon using the details below. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online. Appointments for our kids services should be booked by a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
