Who We Are

Our Story

La Belle was born from a simple belief: beauty rituals should feel like a retreat, not a routine. At our nail salon on Rayford Road in Spring, TX, every detail — from our hand-selected products to the calm of our pedicure lounge — is designed with one goal in mind: helping you leave feeling like the best version of yourself.
